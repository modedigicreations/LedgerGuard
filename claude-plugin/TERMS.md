## What LedgerGuard does

LedgerGuard reads data from payment gateways and billing platforms you explicitly connect (Stripe, Chargebee, PayStack, Paddle, Braintree), computes revenue-reconciliation reports and anomalies from that data, and lets you draft, approve, and record GAAP-compliant dual-entry journal adjustments. It never moves money, never processes payments, and never writes to an external accounting system (QuickBooks/NetSuite/Xero write-back is not implemented — see the README).

## Your account and credentials
